Title: Municipal Ordinance Effective Text Resolver

Notes/Description:
This Intelligent Contract resolves whether a municipal ordinance amendment controls a requested field on a query day. GenLayer validators independently verify law identity, enactment status, effective-date language, target field, controlling deadline, and immutable source manifests before producing a result. Append-only sealed revisions preserve history; corrected evidence creates a new revision; assessment IDs are idempotent; replay, malformed data, contradictory sources, and superseded formulas fail closed. The deployed Studio Dev contract completed the public E2E matrix on chain 61997, including finalized writes, authorization negatives, replay protection, malformed/PDF/source-drift handling, revision history, and supersession. All evidence is linked from the public repository.
